# Ink density

**What it does:** turns the picture into one layer that says how much ink each spot gets:
black = full ink, white = bare paper. Steps after it (Levels, Pixelate…) work on this layer.

**Analog history:** *To be written with Nick* (a grey scale on a process camera, density
readings on film…).

**What the sliders do physically:**
- **Contrast:** above 1, mid-greys get pushed toward full ink or bare paper; below 1, the
  whole picture moves toward a middle grey.
- **Invert:** ink on the light areas instead (light ink on dark paper).
