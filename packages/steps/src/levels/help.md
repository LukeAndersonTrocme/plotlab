# Levels

**What it does:** sorts every spot of the picture into one of a few bands, from the
lightest (band 1) to the darkest. It's the first step toward topographic bands: each band
can later get its own hatch or dot pattern.

**Analog history:** *To be written with Nick* (posterisation in the darkroom with several
lith exposures, contour lines on topographic maps…).

**What the settings do physically:**
- **Bands:** how many tones survive. Fewer bands = bolder, more graphic.
- **Cut by:**
  - *Equal area* gives each band about the same share of the picture, so no band is
    almost empty (a big white background just becomes the lightest band).
  - *Equal steps* cuts the tone scale evenly: 0–20 %, 20–40 %…
  - *My own cut points* lets you type where bands change, as ink percentages.
- **Smooth first:** blurs the picture slightly before cutting, so band edges are smooth
  curves instead of speckled pixels.
